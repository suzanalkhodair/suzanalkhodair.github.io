Prepare Your Research for Publication with Confidence

Publishing your research is a significant achievement, and your manuscript deserves expert editorial support before submission. I provide professional medical editing, proofreading, and writing services to help researchers communicate their work with clarity, accuracy, and confidence. I also provide translation between English and Arabic in any health field.

Whether you are preparing a journal article, thesis, dissertation, research proposal, or conference presentation, I can ensure that your writing meets the highest academic and publishing standards.

My name is Dr Suzan Alkhodair. I am a physician and board-certified editor in the life sciences with over 10 years of professional experience. Over the past decade, I have helped medical researchers, health care professionals, postgraduate students, and academics strengthen their manuscripts for publication in national and international journals.

Manuscript Editing/Proofreading

From original articles to practice guides, each manuscript is carefully reviewed and edited to improve the following:

  • scientific clarity and logical flow
  • language, grammar, spelling, and punctuation
  • academic writing style and tone
  • terminology and consistency
  • readability for an international audience
  • compliance with ICMJE recommendations, reporting guidelines, and journal instructions

I am experienced with

  • ICMJE Recommendations
  • AMA style (11th edition)
  • APA style (7th edition)
  • Vancouver, APA, ACS, and other major referencing systems

Medical Writing

Research and clinical work often leave little time for writing. There are many ways I can help you get your paper ready without delay. For example, I can

  • Complete sections of your manuscript,
  • Write the abstract, a plain-language summary, or highlights,
  • Adapt your thesis into a journal article,
  • Rewrite your paper to fit a journal’s word count, or
  • Prepare educational or teaching material for different audiences.

If you have other writing tasks that I can assist you with. Get in touch and let's discuss them.
